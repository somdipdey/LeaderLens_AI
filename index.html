MBA Capstone Research Prototype

LeaderLens AI

A privacy-preserving self-reflection and improvement tool for dark traits, ethical leadership, and AI-assisted development planning.

Privacy-first design No account. No server. No tracking. Results stay in your browser unless you choose to export or share them.

Purpose of this tool

LeaderLens AI supports individual self-reflection on four Dark Tetrad tendencies—narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy, and sadism—alongside three light/ethical leadership capacities: empathy, humility, and ethical leadership.

The aim is developmental: the tool helps you notice behavioural tendencies, generate a structured reflection prompt for a GenAI assistant such as Copilot or ChatGPT, and create a safe 2–4 week improvement plan.

Important: This is not a clinical, diagnostic, recruitment, performance-management, or disciplinary tool. Scores are reflective indicators only. Use them to guide learning, not to label yourself or others.

How to use it

  1. Complete the Pre‑Survey to capture your baseline self-perception.
  2. Complete the Self‑Assessment to view dark and light trait profiles.
  3. Use Results & AI Plan to copy a structured prompt into Copilot or ChatGPT.
  4. Follow the AI-assisted development plan for 2–4 weeks.
  5. Complete the Post‑Survey and export the anonymous comparison summary if requested by the researcher.

Research basis

The tool is informed by established constructs in Dark Triad/Tetrad research, ethical leadership, empathy, humility, and trustworthy AI governance. It uses original research-prototype items rather than copyrighted questionnaire items.

Interpretation guide

  • Dark traits: higher scores are reflective signals, not labels. Focus on behavioural triggers and safeguards.
  • Light traits: higher scores indicate self-reported strengths. Lower areas can guide development priorities.
  • Mixed profiles are normal: people can show both risk tendencies and ethical strengths depending on context.

Safe use principles

  • Do not share your scores if you feel pressured.
  • Do not use scores to judge colleagues.
  • Use GenAI advice as suggestions only.
  • Seek appropriate support if the exercise causes distress.
